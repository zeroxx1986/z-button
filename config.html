Talks to the device over USB (Web Serial). Needs a secure context: an https:// page or localhost. A page opened straight off disk will not work.

Nothing is stored until you save it. Enter or Save stores one setting on the device, Clear restores its default, and a checkbox saves as soon as you toggle it. Reload config reads the settings back from the device.

Actions

Firmware update

Sends the image to the other slot, one chunk at a time, waiting for the device to acknowledge each chunk. Keep this page open until the device reboots.

Log

Goes to the device as one line, exactly as the console would receive it. The device echoes it back into the log.
